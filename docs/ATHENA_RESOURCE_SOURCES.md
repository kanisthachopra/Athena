# Athena starter resource evidence

Checked 8 October 2026. This is a **V — limited public-source check**, with **H — editorial browsing choices** explicitly identified. It is not clinical review, a validated curriculum, an endorsement by the publishers, or evidence of completed product integration. It follows the owner-approved resource-first ages 0–6 scope and free-first policy in the decision register.

## What was checked

The 22 records in `lib/athena/resource-catalog.ts` link to publisher-hosted pages, not invented resources. The research tool retrieved page text, resource metadata and the access links described below without a paid account. “Free” describes the linked item inspected, not every service sold by that publisher. No payment or account enrollment was performed. A successful text retrieval does **not** prove audio/video playback, every download, accessibility, lawful embedding, or access from an Indian residential network. Those remain separate checks.

`checkedAt` records this public-page check, not expert approval. Descriptions are short original navigation summaries, not reproduced articles. `minMonths` and `maxMonths` are browsing filters; the accompanying `ageNote` states when they are Athena's inference. No inferred range is a milestone or a guarantee of suitability. Ages given for parent preparation concern the child's context, not permission to put an infant in front of a screen.

## Language coverage

The selector has English plus Mandarin Chinese, Japanese, French, Spanish, German, Korean, Italian, Portuguese, Arabic and Russian, as approved. This starter catalog now has at least one individually inspected free resource page for **English and all ten foreign languages**. This is starter-link coverage, not equal depth or a complete birth-to-six language curriculum. All added picture books have a publisher reading level of 1, not a child-age rating; their 3–6 shared-reading filter is explicitly an Athena curation choice. Infant/toddler language shelves can therefore remain empty. A search result in a selected language must still be checked for age, access and actual content.

[Storybooks Canada's language directory](https://www.storybookscanada.ca/about/languages/) lists all eleven, but explicitly says some translations and recordings are incomplete. Its [download directory](https://www.storybookscanada.ca/downloads/) likewise distinguishes available text from audio. Do not infer that every story has usable narration in every language, or that a parent can model unfamiliar pronunciation without support. Story translations are not equivalent to a language curriculum or evidence that a child should learn eleven languages at once. Variety-specific Arabic and Portuguese metadata remains to be researched.

- [French story illustration](https://www.storybookscanada.ca/images/0327/01.jpg): Rob Owen, African Storybook, via Storybooks Canada. The [story page](https://www.storybookscanada.ca/stories/fr/0327/) identifies the same license.

The Korean and Arabic reading editions link to the same first image and each independently states CC BY 3.0. The Italian counting edition links to the same second image and states CC BY 3.0. Those three edition pages and their image links were checked separately; reuse is not inferred merely from their translated titles. Each catalog record carries the artist credit and license URL.

Render the credit, source and license link whenever showing these images; preserve the image without alterations, or accurately disclose any crop/change. No image was downloaded or copied into the repository. Other thumbnail fields are intentionally absent: a public image URL alone does not establish reuse rights. The Japanese, Spanish, Portuguese and Russian stories specify a noncommercial license; their images are not imported. A generated publisher/topic cover must be labelled as an Athena cover, never a screenshot of the resource. Publisher logos, screenshots, video copying and full-text republication are not authorized by this catalog.

## Remaining checks

1. Verify actual reader/video loading from the product and an India connection; link out cleanly when embedding is restricted.
2. Review complete source text before producing an AI summary; snippets and collection descriptions cannot justify a summary of unseen videos or PDFs.
3. Expand independently inspected language resources and early-infancy coverage. Do not pad empty age/domain cells with mismatched resources.
4. Add resource-specific accessibility, captions, duration and image permissions as observed evidence, not guessed defaults.
5. Keep explicit family choice and stop/skip options. These pages are educational sources, not medical advice or instructions to achieve a developmental score.
