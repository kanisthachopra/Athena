# Production direction

## Direction contract

THESIS: Parents arrange optional experiences in clearly named spaces. No scores, completion pressure, decorative connector diagrams or app-wide top navigation.

OWN-WORLD: Owner-approved Studio composition with Plum & Porcelain, Lato, a persistent left rail, restrained plum surfaces and operational controls. No illustration assets.

STORY: Today offers one optional experience; Week makes destinations and swaps explicit; Guide answers without authoritative writes. Parents can inspect their saved profile and source observations.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Week has a left rail, family/page breadcrumb, child heading and plan selector, then named day spaces. Each experience has preparation, explanation and move/replace controls. Phones use a menu and one-column spaces.

FORM: Spatial Workspace, explicitly selected by the owner from four interactive suites. Seed: owner-pinned; code-led implementation of the selected interactive prototype, not a new visual tournament. Public home, login and email confirmation omit added visuals.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Verification scope

Week's signature is a 420ms finite FLIP after a successful guarded server mutation. No optimistic false success. Reduced-motion, local setting and hidden-page cancellation. New SQL must be applied and tested before move/undo is called verified. Runtime AI configuration and content review are unchanged. The source of truth's wider release gates remain open. Existing family data must not be copied into reports or committed screenshots.
